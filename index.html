How to Play

You're running a small regional economy against a rival region. Whoever builds the higher total GDP by the last turn wins.

The Three Sectors

  • Primary — Farms and Mines dig up or grow raw materials.
  • Secondary — Factories turn raw materials into goods.
  • Tertiary — Shops sell finished goods to customers for cash.

Terrain Key

Grass — buildable land for Farms, Factories and Shops.

Hill — rocky land, only Mines can be built here.

River — can't be built on, but Farms next to it resist droughts.

Road — connects your buildings so goods can actually move.

Roads Matter

A Factory only works if a road path connects it to a Farm or Mine. A Shop only works if a road path connects it to a Factory. A disconnected building shows dimmed and produces little or nothing — check the small ring under each building: green means efficient, yellow is struggling, red is barely working.

Keep It Balanced

Too many Farms with too few Factories means raw materials pile up unsold. Too many Factories with too few Shops means goods pile up unsold. Watch the Sector Balance bars in-game to stay even.

Each Turn

A random event may hit (drought, festival, breakdown...). Spend cash building or laying roads. Press End Turn to watch goods move and see your report. Harder mode adds loans you can borrow and must repay with interest.
